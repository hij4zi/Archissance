Bellflower Residences

Bellflower Residences brings eight two-story homes together in a cohesive residential development that blends Mediterranean-inspired character with a comfortable suburban scale.

A-19  ·  Eight-home for-sale community  ·  Bellflower, California

The homes are organized as individual residences with private driveways and two-car garages, creating a clear sense of ownership while maintaining a unified streetscape. Stucco walls, warm clay tile roofs, stone veneer accents, and modest arched openings establish the architectural language, while varied rooflines, recessed entries, balconies, and projecting garage volumes give each home depth and distinction. Landscaping between the residences softens the street edge and reinforces the neighborhood character, resulting in a compact development that feels residential, approachable, and visually consistent without becoming repetitive.
