Irvine Residence Interior

A gut renovation of a tower condominium, opened up to the glass so the city view is the first thing you see from the entry and the last thing from the kitchen.

A-20  ·  High-rise condominium — full interior  ·  Irvine, California

The original plan boxed the living space away from the windows. The renovation removes those walls, drops a long island where a partition used to be, and lines the kitchen up so the view runs uninterrupted from the front door to the far glass.

The palette is deliberately quiet — white lacquer, book-matched quartz, warm brass — so the changing light off the windows carries the room. A glass-walled study borrows that light without giving up acoustic separation.

Every surface, fixture and lighting position was drawn; the contractor built from a full interior set.
