J & Huss Custom Jewelry

J & Huss Custom Jewelry transforms a compact mall tenant space into a refined, contemporary showroom that uses transparency, lighting, and carefully detailed displays to give the brand a stronger retail presence.

A-07  ·  Mall jewelry showroom tenant improvement  ·  San Jose, California

Located at Eastridge Mall in San Jose, the approximately 789 SF tenant improvement introduces a new storefront, updated lighting, floor finishes, and custom jewelry fixtures within the existing shell. The design pairs a white brick façade with black metal framing and expansive glazing to create a crisp, highly visible storefront, while the interior uses a restrained palette of warm neutrals, dark accents, illuminated wall displays, and glass showcases to keep the jewelry as the primary focus. Raised soffits and layered lighting help define key display and service areas, while selective HVAC modifications and the reuse of the existing ceiling allow the renovation to work efficiently within the existing building conditions.
