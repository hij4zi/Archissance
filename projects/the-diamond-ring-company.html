The Diamond Ring Company

The Diamond Ring Company transforms a 2,310 SF mall tenant space into a refined jewelry showroom centered on visibility, elegance, and a strong luxury retail identity.

A-06  ·  Mall jewelry showroom tenant improvement  ·  San Jose, California

Located at Westfield Valley Fair in San Jose, the tenant improvement introduces a new storefront and a complete interior refresh while retaining portions of the existing ceiling structure. The design is organized around illuminated glass display cases, dark wood cabinetry, polished flooring, and a restrained black, white, and champagne-gold palette that gives the showroom a sophisticated, gallery-like character. A geometric gold feature wall and coordinated storefront surround reinforce the brand at key focal points, while layered lighting, mirrored elements, and crystal chandeliers add depth and brightness throughout the space. The project also includes updated lighting, modified HVAC distribution, raised soffits, new floor finishes, and new store fixtures, resulting in a cohesive retail environment designed to showcase the jewelry as the primary visual focus.
